About CPO & RPO Delhi

Regional Passport Office, Delhi

Central Passport Organization (CPO), Ministry of External Affairs, Govt. of India

The Central Passport Organization (CPO) was created in 1959 as a subordinate office of the Ministry of External Affairs. It is tasked with the issuance of Passports and Travel Documents to citizens of India and maintaining cadre records of all passport officers and assistants across India.

The Regional Passport Office (RPO) Delhi administers NCT Delhi and designated National Capital Region areas through its Main Office in Bhikaji Cama Place, multiple Passport Seva Kendras (PSKs), and Post Office Passport Seva Kendras (POPSKs).

This automated e-Service Book Portal maintains continuous digital records of staff appointments, annual increments, leave balances (EL, HPL, CCL) under Central Civil Services (CCS) Leave Rules 1972, and superannuation schedules.
